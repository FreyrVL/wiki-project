# LoTR/The Hobbit races/species
The world of J.R.R. Tolkien knows a plethora of races and species, not all of which live together in peace and happiness.
Below you find a list of the races mentioned in his works.

## Races/species

- Maiar
- Valar
- Balrog
- Elves
- Men
- Ents
- Dwarves
- Hobbits
- Nazgûl/Ring Wraiths
- Ghosts/shades/the dead
- Orcs
- Trolls
- Barrow-wights
- Giants
- Dragons
